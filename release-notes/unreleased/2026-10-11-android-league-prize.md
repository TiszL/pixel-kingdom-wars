---
title: A season PRIZE tab on the Android Leagues card
category: new
platforms: android
requires: none
---
The Leagues card in the Android app has a new PRIZE tab with a countdown to the end of the weekly season, the kingdoms that would place if it ended now, ranked by this week's trophy change from rated raids, and your own place or what your kingdom still needs. The prize pot is not set yet, so the tab shows each band's share without amounts, and nothing is claimed or paid from it. The League and Rewards tabs now show each league's trophy range, the Shards for your Keep level and the item rewards instead of saying they are not set.
