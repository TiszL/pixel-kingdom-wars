---
title: Gems: the new in-game currency
category: new
platforms: all
requires: GEMS_ENABLED
---
Gems replace KEEP as the currency the game charges for troops, heroes, purchases and other in-game costs. Convert the KEEP you hold in the game into Gems one for one at the Gold Forge, and turn Gems into Shards one for one when you need to build. KEEP in your wallet is not touched, and devnet Gems have no monetary value.
