category: improved

Training confirms the server's price, says why a troop cannot be queued and opens an info card for each troop, and saved armies can be loaded, saved, replaced or deleted right on the Barracks sheet. The raid chooser previews the army that is ready right now and names the campaign act, chapter and stages, and running short of energy opens Supplies. Hero skills show their artwork with the catalogue's exact numbers, and the defence log, revenge list, hero gear and scouting all use the new design.
