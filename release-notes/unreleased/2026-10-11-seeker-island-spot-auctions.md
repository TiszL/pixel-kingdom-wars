---
title: Seeker Island spot auctions open in the Android app
category: new
platforms: android
requires: none
---
Seeker Island's 100 trading spots, 20 of them King spots, are now auctioned on Solana devnet for seven-day terms, with 24 hours of bidding for each term. In the Android app, tap Seeker Island on the map or the SKR tile in your wallet to see the time left, your spots, your bids and any outbid alerts, then enter the island to find any spot by number with its top bid and your own. Bids are paid in test SKR, a devnet token with no monetary value, from your linked wallet; the opening bid is 10 test SKR, you approve each bid in your own wallet, and raising your own bid adds only the difference.
