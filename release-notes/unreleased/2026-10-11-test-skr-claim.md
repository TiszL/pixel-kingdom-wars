---
title: Claim test SKR once a day in the Android app
category: new
platforms: android
requires: DEVNET_SKR_FAUCET_ENABLED
---
From Android app 0.7.1, a kingdom that owns land can claim 1,000 test SKR once a day from the DAILY TEST SKR row on the Wallet tab of your Profile, for bidding on Seeker Island spots. Test SKR is a devnet token with no monetary value, you pay the small network fee from your wallet, and the faucet pauses for the day once its daily budget is spent.
