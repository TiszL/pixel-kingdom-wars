---
title: A new Clan card
category: improved
platforms: android
requires: none
---
The Clan Hall now opens your clan on one card with its members, an invite code you can copy and LEAVE CLAN, and a leader can remove a member there. Without a clan you can browse every clan, and long clan names now wrap instead of being cut off. Joining a clan, invitations and promotion are not available in the Android app yet, and existing clans stay as they are.
