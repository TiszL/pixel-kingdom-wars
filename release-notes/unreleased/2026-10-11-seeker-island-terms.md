title: Seeker Island bidding, withdrawals and terms
category: world
platforms: android

While your bid leads it stays locked in the spot's escrow, and if another player outbids you, an alert takes you to that spot, where WITHDRAW returns the whole amount to your wallet or you can raise again. Each round is settled on chain after bidding closes, the top bidder then holds the spot for its seven-day term and the app announces a spot you won, while a spot with no bid stays empty for that term. The next term's bidding runs during the last day of the current one, and claiming test SKR inside the app is not open yet.
