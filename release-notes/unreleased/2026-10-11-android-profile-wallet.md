---
title: A new Profile card with your kingdoms and wallet
category: improved
platforms: android
requires: none
---
The menu now opens your Profile card with Profile, Kingdoms, Wallet and Account tabs, which shows what it last knew at once and stays filled while it refreshes. The Wallet tab shows onchain balances only, now lists every token the server reports for your wallet after the SKR and KEEP tiles, and no longer lets one unreadable row hide the rest. The title screen, sign-in choices, code entry and Seeker check share the new design, and the Solana logo is drawn crisp at every size.
