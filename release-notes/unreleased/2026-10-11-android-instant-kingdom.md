---
title: Your kingdom on screen from the very first frame
category: improved
platforms: android
requires: none
---
The Android app keeps a picture of your kingdom from your last visit, so a cold start can open straight on your own base while the live scene loads underneath, and the picture is deleted when you sign out. Returning to Home from the map, a menu or a battle shows your base at once, the scene clears to the realm's ground colour instead of black, and leaving the globe never flashes an empty frame. Opening the map shows the globe's own sky with the loading strip at the bottom, and a loading line without real progress sweeps steadily instead of sitting half full.
