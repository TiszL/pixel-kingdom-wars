---
title: A new build tray with each building's own artwork
category: improved
platforms: android
requires: none
---
The BUILD tray has its own tabs for traps, heroes and supplies, and every card shows the building's artwork with its count, limit, cost, build time and Keep level from the server, showing unknown rather than a guessed zero. Prices stay on their cards during a refresh, and a building that is not offered stays listed as unavailable rather than disappearing. While placing, a see-through ghost shows when a tile is taken, each construction site shows exactly one builder, and a BUILD tap that cannot open says why.
