---
title: Smoother battles, and raid requests that no longer get stuck
category: improved
platforms: android
requires: none
---
Live battles use a redesigned HUD and deploy tray and end on a new result card with every number from the server, and battles, live watching, scouting and replays open with a short fade over your kingdom or the loading artwork instead of a blank screen. A raid request the server turns down for good is now cleared instead of staying stuck, BACK TO BASE always takes you home, and a saved raid request can be discarded after a confirmation. A raid the server did create still appears as an unfinished raid you can resume.
