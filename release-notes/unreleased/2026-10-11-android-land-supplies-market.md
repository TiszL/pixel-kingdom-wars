---
title: Land, supplies and Market purchases on clear seals
category: improved
platforms: android
requires: none
---
Buying land now walks through a realm picker, where SETTLE IN reserves the first free tile or you choose one on the map, then a seal with the server's exact price and a finished card. The BUILD tray's Supplies tab lists the game's SOL and KEEP offers at their real prices, and shields, builders and raid energy are confirmed on a seal right over your base. The Market opens on a new sheet with its stalls and your own stock, item purchases show their exact price on a seal before your wallet opens, and player listings are not open yet.
