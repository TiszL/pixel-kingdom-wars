---
title: A redesigned world map with realm and island panels
category: world
platforms: android
requires: none
---
MAP now fills the screen with a new top bar, zoom and ME buttons, a realm rail with the islands first, kingdom filters and a search drawer that remembers your recent searches. Every globe and realm panel opens as a half sheet, your own kingdom wears a gold YOU plate, and tapping another kingdom opens a card with SCOUT and ATTACK. The islands now appear in the globe's very first frame with smooth glows at every zoom, and the Solana Island and Divine Isle panels say plainly what is not open yet.
