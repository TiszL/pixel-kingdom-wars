---
title: Battles start when the first moves arrive, at normal speed
category: fixed
platforms: android
requires: none
---
From Android app 0.7.1, a live battle no longer shows your troops standing still and then rushing forward. The field waits for the first moves from the server, then plays at normal speed a fixed moment behind the server clock, so what you see is always the real battle, and the last seconds play out after the result card.
