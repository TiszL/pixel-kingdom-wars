category: improved

Upgrading a row of walls now shows your balance, the row's total and why any wall is skipped before you confirm, and removing an obstacle, hiring builders and expanding the Keep's land each have their own clear sheet. A paid finish shows its exact currency and cost on its review before anything is spent. The Daily Cache lights exactly the days you have claimed, the Keep's STORES button opens your inventory in place, and the layout editor has the new look.
