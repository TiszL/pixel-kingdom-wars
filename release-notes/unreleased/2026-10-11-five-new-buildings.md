---
title: Five new buildings: Workshop, Lodge, Market, Harvester and Berth
category: new
platforms: all
requires: PHASE_E_BUILDINGS_ENABLED
---
Your base can now hold five new buildings, each placed for 2,000 Shards: the Workshop from Keep level 3, the Lodge and the Market from Keep level 4, and the Harvester and the Berth from Keep level 5. Each one opens its own screen once it is built, and the Android app draws them with their own artwork.
