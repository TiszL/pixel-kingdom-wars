---
title: A redesigned Home screen in the Android app
category: improved
platforms: android
requires: none
---
Home in the Android app has a new heads-up display with your level and league crest, your Shards beside the KEEP and SKR in your linked wallet, quick buttons for Quests, the Daily Cache, Mail and Chat, and ATTACK, ARMY, MAP and BUILD at hand. Tapping a building now frames it in gold with its name and a short bar of its own commands, and a second tap runs the main one. Routine refreshes now happen quietly, so open menus no longer close and taps are no longer lost while your base updates.
