---
title: Checked Seeker Island bids and private leaders
category: new
platforms: android
requires: none
---
Before your wallet opens, the Android app checks that each bid or withdrawal goes only to the Seeker Island program and the test SKR token, and a bid shows as sent only once it is final on chain. An interrupted send is checked before anything is signed again, and the island refreshes on its own while open, with RETRY when it cannot be reached. The leading bidder appears as you or as a Seeker player, never as a wallet address.
