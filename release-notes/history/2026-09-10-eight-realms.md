The globe is now a fantasy world of eight realms, from Crownlands at its heart to Stormcairn’s
highlands, Thornwake’s jungle and Cinderfall’s ashlands. Existing bases moved to Crownlands with
their owners and progress unchanged, and every base now stands in ground painted for its realm.
