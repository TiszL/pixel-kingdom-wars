---
title: Mail, chat, quests and settings on the new design
category: improved
platforms: android
requires: none
---
Mail opens letters on a new sheet where you claim attachments and archive what you have read, and chat slides in as a drawer from its own tab, and each reward letter is claimed on its own. Quests open right over your base with Daily and Kingdom tabs that stay on screen while they refresh, so claiming a quest no longer reloads your kingdom, and each claim is sent only once. Settings, Feedback and battle History share the same design, and game notices appear as short toasts.
