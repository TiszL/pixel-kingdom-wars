A required update: the game now runs on the new battle simulator, so every earlier app version must update before it can fight or build again. It brings the whole new interface built for this season: the redesigned keep and build screens, the Workshop, the Lodge with its expeditions and harvesters, the resource stores and Gems, the Seeker Island spot auctions and realm permits, and the reworked Army, Mail and Clan screens. Updating from inside the app keeps your kingdom and wallet exactly as they are, and the app shows version 0.7.0, matching the web game.
