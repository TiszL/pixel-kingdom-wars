---
title: Full-screen cards on phones, with nothing overlapping
category: improved
platforms: android
requires: none
---
On a phone, every large screen, from the build tray and army to Clan, Leagues and the Gold Forge, now opens as one full-screen card instead of a panel over your base, with your resources on a slim shelf above it and the rest of the HUD out of the way. Quick confirmations rise as half sheets, and on a short window such as a phone turned sideways the header scrolls away while the action buttons stay in reach. The old landscape lock setting is gone, and the game now follows your phone's own rotation setting.
